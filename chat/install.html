Install the app

On iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Open the Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open the installed app and sign in to your inbox.

On Android or desktop, use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home Screen option if an install button isn’t shown here.

Turn on notifications

Inside your inbox, tap Enable notifications and allow the request. On iPhone and iPad, do this from the installed app. Installation alone does not enable notifications.
